Schedule

The Assembly is held in person in Bangor, Maine and virtually over Zoom.

Delegates were chosen randomly from the pool of volunteers. Here is what selected delegates can expect across the Assembly — two intensive days together in Bangor, bracketed by virtual sessions.

Times are subject to small adjustments; delegates receive a detailed agenda before each session.
