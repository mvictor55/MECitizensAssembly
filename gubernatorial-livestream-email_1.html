The Assembly meets the candidates for governor

The Assembly set out to answer one question: "What actions should our next governor and our legislature prioritize to improve PK-12 education in Maine?"

In August, the 64 delegates ratified their answer. Now they're taking it to the people running for governor. Over the next two weeks, delegates will present the Assembly's priorities to each candidate in a separate session.

Each session will be livestreamed at maineassembly.org/watch, and the recordings will stay posted there afterward.
